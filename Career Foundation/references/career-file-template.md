## 3. Wins & Projects

Grouped under the role they happened in. This section is a data-capture layer,
not a writing layer — resist the urge to polish here. Polishing happens in
downstream skills, once the raw material is complete and correct.

### [Company] — [Project/win title]
**Capture status:** raw, unconfirmed (from uploaded document) / raw (from
conversation) / detail complete / metrics confirmed

- **Situation:** What was going on. The context, the problem, the stakes.
- **Task:** What they were specifically responsible for or asked to do.
- **Action:** What they actually did, step by step. Decisions made, people
  involved, tools used. This is usually where the most detail belongs.
- **Result:** What happened. Numbers wherever they exist.
- **Metrics:** Pull the hard numbers out separately so they're easy to scan —
  e.g. "$6.45M pipeline," "50% conversion improvement," "1,000 registrations."
  If there are no hard numbers, say so explicitly rather than leaving blank —
  a confirmed "no metrics available" is different from "not asked yet."
- **Skills demonstrated:** tags — e.g. cross-functional leadership, budget
  ownership, crisis pivot, vendor negotiation.
